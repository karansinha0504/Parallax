# Decisions, supersessions and ruled-out ideas

## Superseded (do NOT revert)
- Glass-letters wordmark intro and the pane-behind-letters version -> replaced by the simple intro (panes drift in, wordmark sharpens beneath, fade to home). Wordmark now in the app's Heros sans, not custom lettering.
- Coloured/tinted glass on UI -> plain liquid glass only (logo panes are the only coloured glass).
- Landscape art covering 3/4 of the library screen -> now about half, uncropped.
- Circular fan on collections -> sideways stack like the logo's panes.
- Big elliptical glow -> card-shaped glow.
- Entertainment/Music tabs at the top of the phone -> bottom dock.
- Abrupt expand when swiping sections -> continuous morph.
- Library cards showing only part of a row -> whole rows only.

## Hardware and platform
- Start on the onn box. If the glass effects struggle, a Google TV Streamer (USB-C hub with power pass-through; exFAT drive) is the fallback. Nvidia Shield ruled out (price in India). Building his own box ruled out (no Widevine L1, so no HD Netflix/Prime).
- No system updates on the onn box, ever.

## Content and legality notes
- Artwork in the personal mockups came from his own library screenshots; keep any such mockups private.
- Downloading music from YouTube downloaders is not legal even if common; the app does not do that. His workflow adds files himself.
